# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: hiring managers, tech leads and recruiters at companies looking for a junior-to-mid frontend / fullstack JS/TS developer (Vue/Nuxt, React/Next.js). They skim on a laptop between other candidates, give a portfolio well under a minute, and decide whether to open the CV or write.
Secondary: small Czech businesses (cafés, studios, shops) who want a website or e-shop built; they need plain language and proof that the result works.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Miloslav Hříbal (hribal.site). Success = a recruiter or client leaves the first viewport knowing who he is, what he builds, and that real people use it, then contacts him or downloads the CV.

## Positioning
Not a tutorial-project portfolio: two paid production frontends for the Minecraft community (SkinsMC, Minecraft IP List) used by millions of players, a shipped client e-shop with Czech QR payments and Fakturoid invoicing, and a nearly finished cross-platform app (BudBuddy) built solo end to end.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4, standalone Docker build; cs/en i18n via cookie + Accept-Language; contact form via Nodemailer; PostHog analytics through /ingest proxy. All must keep working.
- /ukazky/dos-mundos showcase page exists; Montana Cans static export served under /montana.
- Project order (user decision 2026-10-13): Týnky Bordel e-shop, BudBuddy second, paid Minecraft work emphasized above personal projects, Dos Mundos and Montana Cans concept redesigns last.

## Evidence on Hand
- Live sites: tynkybordel.shop, budbuddy.hribal.site, skinsmc.org, minecraftiplist.com, questieapp.com, montana.hribal.site.
- Screenshots / thumbnails in public/ and public/showcase/.
- Employment: SkinsMC (2024–2025), Minecraft IP List (2023–2025), small team of 3–4; freelance since 2025; INFIS Pilsen 2020–2024.
- No testimonials, client logos, or traffic analytics exports on hand; do not invent them. "Millions of users" refers to the Minecraft platforms' audience.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Miloslav Hříbal, domain hribal.site, based in Pilsen, Czech Republic.
- Must not read as an AI-generated / "vibecoded" template portfolio.
- Claims must be defensible in a technical interview (user is honest-junior level; frame strongly, never fabricate skills).

## Product Principles
1. Show the work before talking about it; real screenshots of real products lead.
2. Paid, production work outranks personal and concept work in placement.
3. Every claim points at something a visitor can click and verify.
4. Recruiters first, clients second — but the client path stays one click away.
